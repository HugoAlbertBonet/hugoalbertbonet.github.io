## The problem

Answering multiple-choice questions about biomedical images.

## What I built

A benchmark of vision-language models on PMC-VQA, including LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen2.5-VL-7B.

## Results

The fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-7B reached 60% accuracy, beating a fine-tuned ViLT (36%) and OpenAI o4-mini (56%).
