## What we built

With two co-authors, classical, ensemble and transformer models to detect, classify and quantify hurtful humor in tweets, for the HUHU shared task at IberLEF 2023.

## Publication

**H. Albert Bonet**, A. Magraner Rincón, A. Martínez López. "Detection, Classification and Quantification of HUrtful HUmor (HUHU) on Twitter Using Classical Models, Ensemble Models, and Transformers." *IberLEF 2023, CEUR Workshop Proceedings*, vol. 3496.
