## What I built

Whisper + NLLB speech-translation systems on CoVoST 2, comparing a cascaded approach with an end-to-end one.

## Results

- The cascade reached 35.2 BLEU vs. 17.7 end-to-end.
- LoRA fine-tuning of NLLB-3.3B raised cascade BLEU from 22.5 to 33.4.
